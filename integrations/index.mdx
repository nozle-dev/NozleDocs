title: Integrations
description: Payment providers you can connect to Nozle
---

Nozle collects payments through the payment provider you connect to your organization. Each organization connects its own provider account, so your customers pay into your account, not Nozle's.

Connect a provider in the dashboard under **Settings > Billing settings > Integrations**. Each provider shows **Connect** until it has a connection, then **Add another**, **Edit** and **Disconnect**. Nozle supports Stripe and Razorpay.

| | [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) | [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay) |
|---|---|---|
| **Credential** | Stripe secret key | Razorpay Key ID and key secret, plus a webhook secret |
| **Currency** | The invoice currency | INR only |
| **Plan checkout** | Stripe-hosted Checkout page (a saved card is charged first) | Razorpay order in the Razorpay checkout modal |
| **Payment links** | Stripe-hosted Checkout page | Razorpay payment link |
| **Webhook** | Nozle registers it right after you connect | You add it in the Razorpay dashboard |
| **Payment confirmation** | Signed webhook only | Signed webhook, and your backend's verify call for an order |
| **Checkout status** | Paid upgrades only, by upgrade ID. It reads the session from Stripe but never settles a payment. A new subscription or invoice checkout has no status call | Every checkout, by checkout ID. It reconciles with Razorpay and settles a captured payment |
| **Renewals** | Automatic charge on the saved card | Automatic only with an auto-pay mandate (UPI, card or bank debit) |
| **Refunds and disputes** | Credit-note refunds and lost disputes | Credit-note refunds and lost disputes |

A customer pays through the provider connection assigned to that customer. A customer without one can pay through the [default payment connection](#default-payment-connection). See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout) for the flow that both providers share, and [Payments](/guides/billing/payments) for how payment events update invoices and subscriptions.

## Default payment connection

_Unreleased._ When a customer with no payment connection pays, Nozle uses the workspace's default connection:

- With one connection, that connection is the default.
- With several, choose the default under **Settings > Billing settings > Integrations**, in **Default payment connection**. Until you choose one, there is no default.

The default applies only when the customer starts the payment: [checkout](/guides/billing/checkout), [pay links](/guides/billing/checkout#pay-links), **Pay** in the [customer portal](/guides/billing/invoices#pay-from-the-customer-portal) and credit top-up purchases. Nozle assigns the customer to the default connection at that moment and creates the customer in the provider. Customers with their own connection keep it.

Automatic charges never use the default. A customer without a connection is not charged automatically, so you can bill some customers outside Nozle.

Without a connection for the customer and without a default, checkout answers `422` `payment_provider_required`. Its `message` names the fix: connect a payment provider, choose the default connection, or set the customer's `billing_configuration.payment_provider_code`.
