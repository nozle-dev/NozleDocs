---
title: Integrations
description: Payment providers you can connect to Nozle
---

Nozle collects payments through the payment provider you connect to your organization. Each organization connects its own provider account, so your customers pay into your account, not Nozle's.

Connect a provider in the dashboard under **Settings > Billing settings > Integrations**. Each provider shows **Connect** until it has a connection, then **Add another**, **Edit** and **Disconnect**. Nozle supports Stripe and Razorpay.

| | [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) | [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay) |
|---|---|---|
| **Credential** | Stripe secret key | Razorpay Key ID and key secret, plus a webhook secret |
| **Currency** | The invoice currency | INR only |
| **Plan checkout** | Stripe-hosted Checkout page (a saved card is charged first) | Razorpay order in the Razorpay checkout modal |
| **Payment links** | Stripe-hosted Checkout page | Razorpay payment link |
| **Webhook** | Nozle registers it right after you connect | You add it in the Razorpay dashboard |
| **Payment confirmation** | Signed webhook only | Signed webhook, and your backend's verify call for an order |
| **Checkout status** | Every hosted checkout, by Checkout Session ID, and paid upgrades by upgrade ID. It reads the session from Stripe but never settles a payment | Every checkout, by checkout ID. It reconciles with Razorpay and settles a captured payment |
| **Renewals** | Automatic charge on the saved card | Automatic only with a UPI AutoPay mandate |
| **Refunds and disputes** | Credit-note refunds and lost disputes | Credit-note refunds and lost disputes |

A customer pays through the provider connection assigned to that customer. At plan checkout, a customer who is not assigned to Razorpay is assigned to a Stripe connection automatically. See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout) for the flow that both providers share, and [Payments](/guides/billing/payments) for how payment events update invoices and subscriptions.
