---
title: Integrations
description: Payment providers you can connect to Nozle
---

Nozle collects payments through the payment provider you connect to your organization. Each organization connects its own provider account, so your customers pay into your account, not Nozle's.

Connect a provider in the dashboard under **Settings > Billing > Integrations**. Nozle supports Stripe and Razorpay.

| | [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) | [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay) |
|---|---|---|
| **Credential** | Stripe secret key | Razorpay Key ID and key secret, plus a webhook secret |
| **Checkout** | Embedded Stripe Checkout Session | Razorpay checkout modal, or a payment link |
| **Webhook** | Nozle registers it for you | You add it in the Razorpay dashboard |
| **Payment confirmation** | Signed webhook | Signed webhook, and your backend's verify call for an order |

Razorpay checkout supports only INR. A customer pays through the provider connection assigned to that customer. See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout) for the flow that both providers share, and [Payments](/guides/billing/payments) for how payment events update invoices and subscriptions.
