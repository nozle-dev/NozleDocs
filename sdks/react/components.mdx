`@nozle-js/react` has ready-made components for pricing pages, checkout, plan changes, billing settings, usage, credits and feature gates. Each component page lists every option, shows the component in all six [themes](/sdks/react/styling#themes), and explains how to feed it live data.

## Where components get their data

| Source | What it gives | Components |
|---|---|---|
| Props you pass | Anything you already have | All components; most take their data only from props |
| `BillingProvider` with `publishableKey` | The public plan catalog and the "Powered by Nozle" answer | [`PricingTable`](/sdks/react/components/pricing-table) without `plans`, [`PlanChangeControl`](/sdks/react/components/plan-change-control) plan details, [`CurrentPlan`](/sdks/react/components/current-plan) limits |
| `BillingProvider` with `createCheckout` | Checkout through your merchant route | [`PricingTable`](/sdks/react/components/pricing-table), [`CheckoutButton`](/sdks/react/components/checkout-button), [`UpgradeButton`](/sdks/react/components/upgrade-button), [`UpgradeModal`](/sdks/react/components/upgrade-modal) |
| `BillingProvider` with `billingEndpoint` | The signed-in customer's subscription, entitlements, credits and usage, served by `createBillingHandler` | [`CurrentPlan`](/sdks/react/components/current-plan) without `subscription`, and the [customer hooks](/sdks/react/hooks#customer-hooks) you pass into the other components |
| Your merchant callbacks | Plan changes, cancellation and the portal session | [`PlanChangeControl`](/sdks/react/components/plan-change-control), [`CancellationControl`](/sdks/react/components/cancellation-control), [`BillingPortal`](/sdks/react/components/billing-portal) |

The browser never holds a secret key. Customer data reaches the components through your backend: `createBillingHandler`, your merchant routes, or the customer-scoped portal session. See [Installation](/sdks/react/installation) and [Provider and hooks](/sdks/react/hooks).

## Loading, empty and error states

Components that show customer data take `isLoading` (or `loading`), `error` and `emptyMessage`, and render a placeholder, a `role="alert"` message, or a `role="status"` message instead of their content. Pass the matching fields of the hook result, for example `isLoading={query.isPending}` and `error={query.error}`. Each component page lists its states.

## Powered by Nozle

`PricingTable`, `PlanComparison`, `CurrentPlan`, `Checkout`, `UpgradeModal` and `BillingPortal` show "Powered by" and the Nozle logo below their content, linking to nozle.dev. Nozle decides this on the server: the publishable-key plan catalog returns `meta.nozle_branding`, and the billing portal reads it from its session. When one of these components renders another, for example a modal inside the portal, only the outer one shows the badge. Without a `BillingProvider`, only `BillingPortal` can show it.

The badge shows by default. If your Nozle plan removes the branding, turn off **Show "Powered by Nozle"** under Developers > Customize SDK > Global settings > Branding. The setting is saved for your organization and applies to every component. On other plans the switch stays off-limits and the badge keeps showing.

If you build your own billing UI from the hooks, `useNozleBranding()` returns the same answer and [`<PoweredByNozle />`](/sdks/react/components/powered-by-nozle) renders the badge.

## Appearance

Every component with a look takes `appearance`, and inherits the one on `BillingProvider`. Each component page lists the element keys it exposes for class names and styles. See [Appearance](/sdks/react/styling).
