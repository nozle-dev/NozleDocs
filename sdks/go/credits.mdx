---
title: "Product credits"
description: "Read exact balances, transfer Entity credits, and consume usage."
---

Product-credit quantities are `nozle.Decimal` strings. Keep their exact decimal representation throughout your application.

```go
systems, err := client.ListCreditSystems(ctx)
balance, err := client.GetEntityCreditBalance(ctx, "workspace_123", "user_42", "ai_credits")
transfer, err := client.AllocateCredits(ctx, "workspace_123", "user_42", "ai_credits", nozle.Decimal("100.000000000001"), "allocate-user-42-v1")
_ = systems
_ = balance
_ = transfer
_ = err
```

`ListCreditSystems` follows every active Core page. Customer and Entity balance and operation methods expose sources, exact amounts, and cursors. `DeallocateCredits` returns eligible credits to the customer; transfer amounts must be positive strings with at most 12 decimal places.

```go
check, err := client.CheckUsage(ctx, nozle.UsageParams{CustomerID: "workspace_123", EntityID: "user_42", FeatureCode: "agent_execution", CreditSystemCode: "ai_credits"})
result, err := client.TrackUsage(ctx, nozle.UsageParams{CustomerID: "workspace_123", EntityID: "user_42", FeatureCode: "agent_execution", CreditSystemCode: "ai_credits"}, "agent-run-0183f")
_ = check
_ = result
_ = err
```

`CheckUsage` is advisory. `TrackUsage` atomically evaluates and deducts credits, records the operation, and requires an idempotency key.
