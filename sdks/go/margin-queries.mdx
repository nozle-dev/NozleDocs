---
title: "Margin queries"
description: "Query margin summary and grouped reports."
---

```go
filters := url.Values{"from_date": {"2026-07-01"}, "to_date": {"2026-07-31"}}
summary, err := client.MarginSummary(ctx, filters)
trend, err := client.MarginTrend(ctx, "day", filters)
_ = summary
_ = trend
_ = err
```

`MarginByCustomer`, `MarginByMetric`, `MarginByPlan`, and `MarginByModel` call their corresponding Engine `/api/v1/margin/*` endpoints. Results are `json.RawMessage`; decode into a shape appropriate for your deployed report schema. Trend granularity accepts `hour`, `day`, `week`, or `month`.
