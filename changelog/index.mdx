## October 2026

### Breaking: Stripe checkout is hosted only (Core 11.5.0, Engine 2.13.0)

_Unreleased_

- **Breaking: every Stripe checkout is a Stripe-hosted Checkout Session.** A new subscription's first invoice, an invoice checkout, a paid plan change, the Entity subscription batch and the retry of a first invoice (`POST /invoices/{id}/retry_payment`) answer `{"type": "hosted", "url", "payment_url", "checkout_id", "invoice_id" or "plan_upgrade_intent_id", "amount_cents", "currency", "expires_at"}`. Redirect the customer to `url`. Embedded Stripe Checkout, the `stripe` result type and every `client_secret` and `clientSecret` are removed. No Stripe publishable key and no Stripe.js are needed on your page. A saved card is still charged first (`completed`, `processing` and `scheduled` results are unchanged), and Razorpay is unchanged. See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout#stripe) and [Create checkout](/api/create-checkout#hosted-stripe-result).
- **`cancel_url` and `{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}`.** `POST /checkout` accepts `cancel_url`, validated like `return_url` and defaulting to it. The Entity subscription endpoints take `return_url` only. A return URL may contain the literal `{CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}`, which Stripe replaces with the Checkout Session ID. A refused `cancel_url` answers `400 invalid_return_url`.
- **Status poll.** A paid upgrade is polled by `plan_upgrade_intent_id`: the hosted result is nested while the session is open, then `completed`, and `status` is `expired` when the Stripe session is no longer open. The hosted `checkout_id` is the Stripe session ID, not a status ID. Nozle sends Adaptive Pricing disabled so the customer is charged in the invoice currency.
- **SDKs (Node 0.20.0, React 0.18.0, Python 0.19.0, Go v0.18.0):** Node `cancelUrl` option, Python `cancel_url=`, Go `CancelURL` and `CheckoutInvoiceWithOptions`; the Go `ClientSecret` field is removed. In React, the components redirect to the hosted page, `Checkout` takes only `checkout={result}`, `useCheckoutSession().startCheckout` replaces `fetchClientSecret`, `onStripeClientSecret` and the Stripe peer dependencies (`@stripe/stripe-js`, `@stripe/react-stripe-js`) go away. Self-hosters run `payment_providers:refresh_stripe_webhook_events` once for the new refund events.
- **Receipt link.** GraphQL `Payment.receiptUrl` returns Stripe's receipt page for a payment, fetched from Stripe on demand and never stored. See [Payments](/guides/billing/payments).

### Workspace billing: card at onboarding, failed payments, suspension on day 7 (Core 11.5.0, Engine 2.13.0)

_Unreleased_

- **Every workspace adds a card at onboarding.** The card is verified on a Stripe-hosted page and nothing is charged. There is no rollout switch: it is always on, and existing workspaces are flagged within the hour. The card page is Stripe-hosted, cards only, with Stripe's reuse agreement text, and a lost webhook is recovered by a server-side check. After the card the AI onboarding opens. Until a card is verified, the dashboard and every API key call (Core REST, account MCP, Engine API, LLM proxy, event ingestion) answer `402` `card_required`. See [Workspace billing](/guides/getting-started/workspace-billing).
- **Automatic upgrades run every hour** and charge the prorated invoice to the saved card at once. Members get emails at 50%, 80%, 90% and 100% of the included events or MRR limit.
- **Failed Nozle invoices are retried on days 1 to 6.** Every member gets an email on day 0, a reminder on day 3 and a last reminder on day 6, with a pay link that needs no login, and the dashboard shows a banner.
- **Suspension on day 7.** A workspace with unpaid Nozle invoices gets `402` `workspace_suspended` (which wins over `card_required`) on events, `/can`, the Engine API, the LLM proxy, REST API keys, the account MCP and Copilot, and the dashboard shows only the Nozle invoices page. Webhooks, the customer portal and payment pages keep working. Events sent during the suspension are refused, not queued. The SDKs throw their API error with `code` and never retry it. See [Errors](/api/errors#workspace-billing-errors) and [The can() endpoint](/guides/entitlements/can-endpoint#unavailable-or-blocked).
- **Resume when the invoices are paid**, then the time your own customers were not billed is billed with the original periods.
- **Stripe refund events.** Webhook endpoints created by Nozle subscribe to `refund.updated` and `refund.failed`. The older `charge.refund.updated` is still accepted for one release. See [Stripe](/integrations/stripe#webhook-setup).

### Know when top-up credits can be spent (Core 11.1.0, Engine 2.12.0)

_Unreleased_

- **New webhook `credit_top_up_purchase.fulfilled`.** Sent once per paid top-up purchase, after the credits are granted and can be spent. Until now nothing told you when that happened: the invoice is paid a moment before the grant. See [Webhook events](/api/webhook-events#credit_top_up_purchasefulfilled).
- **`GET /api/v1/credit-top-up-purchases/{id}` on the Engine.** Returns the purchase with `fulfillment_status` (`pending`, then `fulfilled`) and `fulfilled_at`. Poll it after checkout if you do not use the webhook. Purchases in webhooks and in Core's `GET /credit-top-up-purchases/{id}` carry the same fields.
- **Razorpay checkout status for a top-up** reports `fulfillment_status: succeeded` only once the credits are granted, not when the payment reaches Nozle.
- Docs: [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups#know-when-the-credits-can-be-spent) now tells you to wait for the webhook or poll the purchase instead of refreshing on a paid invoice.

### Breaking Core releases: CORS, voided invoices, plan change default, no former-prefix names, API key display (Core 5.0.0 to 7.0.0)

_Unreleased_

- **Core 5.0.0, breaking: browser calls are allowed only from listed origins.** Core answered every origin with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. It now answers only the exact origins in `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (default: the origin of the dashboard URL) and nothing else, preflight included. The customer portal keeps working on any site: `POST /graphql` with a `customer-portal-token` header is allowed from any origin. Server-to-server calls are not affected. See the Core environment variables and [Authentication](/api/authentication#browser-access-cors).
- **Core 5.1.0: a voided invoice can no longer be paid.** Open Stripe Checkout Sessions and Razorpay orders and links are expired when an invoice is voided, marked as paid, partly paid or reduced by a credit note offset. A payment that still lands on a voided invoice is refunded in full (Stripe and Razorpay), and you get `invoice.payment_failure` with `voided_invoice_payment_refunded`, or `voided_invoice_payment_refund_failed` when the provider refuses the refund. Invoice checkout answers `422 validation_errors` with `amount_below_provider_minimum` when the amount left is under the provider's minimum charge. See [Stripe](/integrations/stripe#checkout-flow) and [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay#refunds-and-disputes).
- **Core 5.2.0: plan changes work without a policy per pair.** The default policy `self_serve_default` upgrades now (prorated) and schedules a downgrade at the end of the period, for public plans. A policy for the pair overrides it. An immediate downgrade without a pair policy is refused with `422 plan_transition_policy_required`, and hidden plans and trials are excluded. The options endpoints list the plans the default allows. See [Plan Transition Policies](/api/plan-transition-policies#the-default-policy).
- **Core 6.0.0, breaking: no former-prefix names in the public API.** REST, GraphQL, webhook payloads, CSV exports and webhook headers carry only the Nozle names (`nozle_id`, `X-Nozle-Signature`). This ends the window that was announced until January 19, 2027, early. nozle-js and nozle-python older than 0.10.0 and nozle-go older than 0.9.1 break on the credit system `id`: upgrade the SDK first. Stripe metadata is `nozle_*`. See [legacy key names](/api/api-standards#legacy-key-names-removed-in-core-600).
- **Core 7.0.0, breaking: API keys are shown once.** The full key is returned only when you create or rotate it; every later read is masked. Store it yourself. See [Authentication](/api/authentication#store-your-key-when-you-create-it).
- **SDKs: choose the subscription in `can()`.** Node 0.17.0 (`options.subscriptionId`, `options.entityId`), React 0.16.0 (`useEntitlement(feature, { subscriptionId, entityId })`), Python 0.17.0 (`subscription_id=`, `entity_id=`, sync and async) and Go v0.16.0 (`CanWithOptions`). A customer with several subscriptions and no selector gets `allowed: false` with `reason: subscription_required`. `track()` without a subscription ID now fails with a 422 `active_subscription_not_found` or `active_subscription_ambiguous` error in all three server SDKs (the Python error had `status_code` 200). See [The can() endpoint](/guides/entitlements/can-endpoint).

### Checkout return, LLM proxy delivery, credits plus money (Engine 2.9.0)

_Unreleased_

- **Checkout return URLs are decided per organization.** The Engine no longer has an allowlist or a default return URL of its own (`CHECKOUT_RETURN_URL` and `CHECKOUT_RETURN_URL_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` are removed from the Engine). `POST /checkout` and `POST /manage-payment-method` send `return_url` to Core, which checks it against your organization's `checkout_return_origins` and uses `checkout_default_return_url` when none is sent. A refused URL answers `400 invalid_return_url`. Before you upgrade, register every origin that only the Engine list allowed. Set both in the dashboard under **Developers > Checkout return**. See [Return URLs](/guides/billing/checkout#return-urls-and-your-organizations-origins).
- **LLM proxy usage is delivered durably.** Each event is stored before the response returns, retried with backoff for about five hours when Nozle's billing service is down, and recorded once per transaction ID. Events are sent with your organization's secret key, not the request's `X-Nozle-Key`. See [LLM Proxy](/api/llm-proxy).
- **A Feature paid with credits cannot also carry a priced charge in the same plan.** `POST /usage/check` and `POST /usage/track` answer `409 feature_billed_in_money` instead of taking credits and billing the usage again. A charge priced at 0 still works with credits. See [Credits or money, not both](/api/usage-track#credits-or-money-not-both).
- Docs: only the **Admin** role can create secret keys. See [Authentication](/api/authentication).

### Plan versioning (API 4.1.0, Engine 2.7.0, Node 0.16.0, React 0.15.0, Python 0.16.0, Go 0.15.0)

_Unreleased_

- **Changed for every organization:** changing what a plan bills or grants while customers pay for it saves the plan's next version under the same code. Existing subscribers keep their version and price; new subscriptions get the current version. Before, the change applied to existing subscribers from their next invoice; to keep doing that, send `disable_version: true` with the edit. Plans have `version` and `is_current`, and `GET /plans` returns current versions only. See [Plan versioning](/guides/billing/plan-versions).
- `PUT /plans/{code}` takes `disable_version` (change the current version for everyone) and `force_version`. An edit of an older version is refused with `409 plan_version_not_current`.
- Sell an older version with `plan_version` on `POST /subscriptions` and on the Engine's `POST /subscribe`. The same plan code sent again for an existing subscription never moves it.
- `GET /plans/{code}/versions` lists the versions; version migrations (`/plans/{code}/version-migrations`: preview, start, list, read, cancel) move one version's subscribers to another at the end of their billing periods, charging nothing when they run. See [Plan Versions and Migrations](/api/plan-versions).
- Settings › Billing: `plan_version_credits` decides what a moved customer keeps of the old version's plan credits.
- New webhook `plan.version_created`. Subscriptions carry `plan_version`, `previous_plan_version`, `next_plan_version` and `plan_version_migration_id`.
- SDKs: plan versions and migrations in `@nozle-js/node`, `nozle-sdk` and `nozle-go`; `<CurrentPlan />` no longer shows the current version's limits to a customer on an older version.

### Engine webhook verification (Engine 2.0.3)

_October 21, 2026_

- Core webhooks to Nozle's Engine are verified with the sending organization's own `hmac_key`. Every organization's `hmac` endpoint is accepted; an unsigned, forged or cross-organization request, or one whose organization is unknown, gets `401` in every environment, and the Engine answers `503` if the key cannot be read so that Core retries. **Engine webhook endpoints must use the `hmac` signature algorithm**: `jwt` endpoints are rejected. - A signed webhook that names another organization's subscription no longer creates that subscription's credit grants.
- The `NOZLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` setting is removed and ignored.

### Engine 2.0.2: subscription selection, running totals and billing periods

_October 21, 2026_

- **Breaking (2.0.0):** `GET /can` accepts optional `subscription_id` and `entity_id`. Without them, a customer with several current subscriptions is evaluated on its one own (not Entity-owned) subscription; when there is none or more than one, it answers `200` with `allowed: false` and `reason: subscription_required`, and `POST /billing/upgrade` answers `409 subscription_required`. Before, both used the most recently started subscription. A customer with one current subscription sees no change. Send `subscription_id` or `entity_id` for customers with several. See [Which subscription is checked](/guides/entitlements/can-endpoint#which-subscription-is-checked).
- Usage counters are kept per subscription, so one Entity's seat never counts against another subscription's limit. Only subscriptions that are current now count.
- Recurring Features (seats, storage) keep one running total across billing periods, so a hard limit no longer reopens on the first day of a period. `/can` reports the real `used` of a metered Feature that is only priced by a charge, with `limit` and `remaining` null. Before it was 0.
- `/can` usage follows the billing period Nozle invoices in, for every plan interval, calendar and anniversary billing, and the customer's time zone (2.0.1). Semiannual plans reset every six months; yearly and semiannual plans that bill charges monthly reset monthly; weekly calendar periods start on Monday; anniversary dates on the 29th to the 31st use the last day of shorter months. See [Usage and billing periods](/guides/entitlements/can-endpoint#usage-and-billing-periods).
- Credit usage (`POST /usage/check` and `POST /usage/track`) accepts every credit rate: an amount that does not end within 12 decimals is rounded up to 12 decimals, so usage is never under-charged (`0.333333333334` for 1 event at 1 credit per 3). Before, it answered `422 credit_usage_configuration_invalid`. A boolean or config Feature answers `422 feature_not_consumable` instead of `500` (2.0.2). See [Amount rounding and Features](/api/usage-track#amount-rounding-and-features).
- One dead-lettered usage event no longer blocks credit usage for the whole organization. `503 credit_usage_publication_backlog` now means 1,000 or more events still being retried, or the oldest older than 5 minutes, and clears by itself. Failed deliveries are retried for about a day before an event is dead-lettered (1.9.1). See [usage track](/api/usage-track#timestamp-and-retries).
- Wrapped LLM calls are costed when the parent Feature has no cost composition (for example an AI-token Cost Sheet with "Linked feature: none"): each cost event is rated on its own meter and attributed to the parent's customer, subscription and Feature, and counts in margin. Cost events recorded before need a replay (parent Feature Event first, then its cost events) (1.9.2). See [AI token billing](/guides/ai/token-billing).
- `GET /analytics/overview` returns invoiced revenue (`invoiced_revenue_cents`, `previous_period_invoiced_revenue_cents`, `invoiced_revenue_mom_percent`, `invoiced_revenue_by_currency`, and the dates of both windows) next to the estimated usage revenue, and month over month compares the same days of the previous month. Before, on October 2 with flat revenue it showed -96.7% (1.10.0). See [Overview revenue](/guides/margin/dashboard).
- Includes Engine 1.9.1, 1.9.2, 1.10.0, 1.11.0, 2.0.0, 2.0.1 and 2.0.2.

### Legacy keys, entitlement fixes and checkout origins (API 3.8.2)

_October 21, 2026_

- **Superseded by Core 6.0.0: the old key names are removed now, not on January 19, 2027.** The next sentence describes the window Core 3.8.2 opened. **The old key names are back until January 19, 2027, then removed.** Since API 1.0.0 the REST, webhook and CSV keys had been renamed from the former product prefix to `nozle_` (`nozle_id`) or to no prefix (`customer_id`) without notice. Until January 19, 2027 (inclusive), REST responses and webhook payloads carry each old key beside its new key, with the same value, on every object that had it; the old EU tax codes resolve to `nozle_eu_*` in `tax_codes` and `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /taxes/{code}`; and CSV exports repeat the id columns at the end under their old names. The `nozle_*` keys stay. **Read the new keys before that date**: after it the old names are removed. The key list is on [API standards](/api/api-standards#legacy-key-names-removal-january-19-2027), and the exact old names are in the OpenAPI specification.
- Entitlement `PATCH` (`/plans/{code}/entitlements` and `/subscriptions/{external_id}/entitlements`) changes only the metered settings it sends. `{"hard_limit": false}` keeps the usage limit, and an omitted `min_margin_percent`, `margin_enforcement_mode` or `enforce_plan_margin` is unchanged. Before, every omitted setting was reset (no usage limit, hard limit, no margin floor, `enforce`). On a subscription, omitted settings keep the subscription's override, or the plan's value. `POST` still replaces the whole entitlement. Restoring a removed feature or dropping an override now reaches `/can` within its usual refresh (3.5.1).
- Onboarding's launch check: GraphQL `Event.matchCustomer` and `Event.matchSubscription` now match events stored in Postgres to their customer and subscription, so a correct event no longer shows "No customer owns this event" (3.5.2).
- A plan change that takes effect now is refused with `422 outstanding_invoice` while the subscription has a paid invoice whose payment was lost in a dispute, and a termination or plan change no longer credits the unused part of such a period (3.5.3). See [Outstanding invoices and lost disputes](/api/subscription-management#outstanding-invoices-and-lost-disputes).
- The finalized revenue of the Cost Intelligence report is by service period, in major units of each currency (JPY was 100 times too low, KWD 10 times too high). `cost` and `finalizedMargin` can be `null`, and the new `costStatus` is `loaded` or `not_loaded` (3.6.0). See [Finalized revenue](/api/cost-intelligence-report#finalized-revenue).
- An immediate upgrade with `proration_behavior: none` that keeps the billing anchor now invoices the current plan for its period up to the change. The new plan's fee starts at the next anchor and no credit note is issued. Before, nothing was billed (3.6.1). See [Proration on a mid-cycle upgrade](/api/plan-transition-policies#proration-on-a-mid-cycle-upgrade).
- Usage thresholds are refused with `422` and `usage_thresholds: [feature_not_available]` on a plan without progressive billing, and lifetime usage answers `403 feature_not_available` without lifetime usage or progressive billing. Before, thresholds were dropped and lifetime usage returned amounts that were never computed (3.7.1). See [Premium features](/guides/getting-started/nozle-plans#premium-features).
- Each organization can register `checkout_return_origins` (up to 20 HTTPS origins) and a `checkout_default_return_url`, through `PUT /organizations` and GraphQL `updateOrganization` (`developers:manage`). Checkout return URLs are accepted on those origins and on the deployment's own. Without a return URL the customer returns to the default URL, else the first origin, else the provider's success URL (3.8.0). See [Return URLs](/guides/billing/checkout#return-urls-and-your-organizations-origins).
- `POST /credit-top-up-purchases` checks the customer's payment provider before it creates anything: a provider error leaves no invoice, purchase or webhook, and a retry creates nothing. A successful purchase writes its `credit_top_up_purchase.created` activity log (3.8.1).
- Automatic dunning runs for organizations whose Nozle plan includes it (Growth and up). A customer or billing entity accepts only a dunning campaign of its own organization (another's answers `not_found`), and applying the billing entity's current campaign again succeeds (3.8.2). See [Payments](/guides/billing/payments).
- Includes API 3.5.1, 3.5.2, 3.5.3, 3.6.0, 3.6.1, 3.7.0, 3.7.1, 3.8.0 and 3.8.1.

### Error statuses and checkout fixes

_October 17, 2026_

- **Breaking:** a request that the resource's current state refuses now returns `422` instead of `405`, with the same `code` (for example `not_voidable` or `plan_transition_policy_required`). A conflict with another operation in progress returns `409`. The SDKs branch on `code`, so their behavior is unchanged. See [Errors](/api/errors).
- Bad bodies and unreachable dependencies return JSON errors with a `code` instead of `500`: `400 bad_request`, `502 upstream_unavailable`.
- Checkout charges only through the connection assigned to the customer, for single and bulk Entity checkout, and returns `422 payment_provider_required` otherwise. Customer create keeps `payment_provider` and `payment_provider_code` without `sync_with_provider`.
- Checking out the same plan again reopens an abandoned new-subscription checkout; a different plan replaces it and returns the new checkout. `409 subscription_checkout_in_progress` (`entity_subscription_checkout_in_progress` for an Entity) remains only while the provider is still processing a payment for the old checkout.
- Stripe: a reused invoice checkout key returns `409 idempotency_key_reused`; a rejected webhook URL sends `payment_provider.error`; `payment_method.detached` clears the stored default. Razorpay credentials are checked when you connect.

### Usage, credits and checkout fixes

_October 17, 2026_

- The [LLM proxy](/api/llm-proxy#subscription) finds the subscription to bill before it calls the provider, and its usage event carries that `external_subscription_id`, so proxy usage is billed. A customer with several active subscriptions needs the new `X-Nozle-Subscription` header, which is not forwarded. A customer with no matching active subscription returns `422 active_subscription_not_found`, several without the header `422 active_subscription_ambiguous`; the provider is not called.
- `POST /events` and `/events/batch` accept `external_customer_id` without `external_subscription_id`: the event is billed to the customer's single active subscription. No or several active subscriptions return `422 active_subscription_not_found` or `422 active_subscription_ambiguous` (in a batch, under the event's position). An event with neither id returns `422 validation_errors` with `external_subscription_id: ["value_is_mandatory"]`. See [Events](/api/events).
- `can()` estimates revenue for the units of the call: 1 for a count Feature, the Feature's `field_name` value in `metadata` for a sum Feature. Other aggregations, or a sum call without that value, report economics `unavailable`, and the margin policy's `on_unavailable` decides. See [economics](/guides/entitlements/can-endpoint).
- The deprecated [Check & Deduct](/api/check-and-deduct) moves the wallet's money balance with the credits: `balance_cents`, the ongoing balance and the consumed amount change by credits × the wallet rate, and the wallet transaction amount is that money amount.
- Paid credit top-ups grant their credits when the top-up invoice is fully paid, by any route: a card or provider payment, a manual payment for the full amount, a payment request, or marking the invoice paid. A partial manual payment grants nothing until the invoice is fully paid. See [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups).
- An unpaid plan or single Entity checkout expires after 23 hours, like an Entity checkout batch: the subscription is canceled with `cancelation_reason: timeout` (`subscription.canceled` webhook), the payment-gated invoice is closed, and the Stripe Checkout Session or Razorpay order expires. The coupons, credit-note credits and wallet credits the invoice used are returned when the checkout expires or its payment fails. See [Unpaid checkout expiry](/api/create-checkout#unpaid-checkout-expiry).
- Stripe invoice checkout and invoice payment URLs refuse an invoice that is paid, voided, draft, closed, failed or has nothing due with `invalid_invoice_status_or_payment_status`, as Razorpay already did.
- A paid upgrade checkout returns `409 subscription_change_pending` while the subscription has a scheduled cancellation or plan change. Cancelling at period end, scheduling a plan change or applying an immediate downgrade cancels an open upgrade checkout and expires its Stripe Checkout Session or Razorpay order.
