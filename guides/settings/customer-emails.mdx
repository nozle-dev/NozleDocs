title: Customer emails
description: Choose which emails Nozle sends to your customers, and where the buttons in them lead.
---

Customer emails are the emails Nozle sends to your customers, in your brand, on your behalf. Open **Settings** and choose **Customer emails**.

Each [billing entity](/guides/settings/billing-entities) has its own switches. Emails go out from Nozle's no-reply address, and replies go to the billing email of the entity. If the entity has no billing email, Nozle sends nothing, whatever is switched on. Every email is off until you turn it on, except the three limit emails below, which are on by default. **Turn on recommended** switches on the ones marked Recommended.

## The emails

| Group | Email |
| --- | --- |
| Billing documents | Invoice finalized, Credit note issued, Payment receipt |
| Payments | Payment failed, Payment needs confirmation, Unpaid invoice reminders (_Unreleased_) |
| Usage and credits | Credits used up, Usage alert, Usage threshold reached, Spending limit almost reached, Spending limit reached, Usage limit almost reached |
| Subscription | Trial ending, Renewal reminder, Price change scheduled, Subscription started, Subscription ended |

_Unreleased (Core 11.16.0, dashboard 2.4.0)._ **Unpaid invoice reminders** (`dunning.step`) sends one email per step of your [dunning policy](/guides/billing/dunning), with the amount, the invoice number, the deadline and the pay link. Through the API, add `dunning.step` to the billing entity's `email_settings` with `PUT /billing_entities/{code}`. When it is on, it replaces the failed payment email (`invoice.payment_failure`) on day 0 for an invoice a dunning policy runs on, so the customer gets one email, not two.

_Core 11.23.0._ **Spending limit almost reached** (`budget.threshold_reached`), **Spending limit reached** (`budget.exceeded`) and **Usage limit almost reached** (`usage_limit.threshold_reached`) tell your customer that their usage reached a threshold of a [spend limit](/guides/entitlements/spend-limits) or of a hard usage limit of their plan. They are on by default: a new billing entity starts with them unless its creation sets `email_settings`, and existing billing entities get them with Core 11.23.1. A sandbox billing entity starts with no email. Switch them off here, or remove them from the billing entity's `email_settings` with `PUT /billing_entities/{code}`.

Payment receipt needs the issue receipts add-on. Without it the switch stays off. **Send test** sends a sample of an email to you.

## Customer billing page

**Customer billing page** is the address the buttons in your emails open. Enter a full address that starts with `https://`. Emails marked **Billing page** have no button until you set it, except Credits used up, which falls back to the hosted portal. Payment buttons open the payment provider's page first.

## Who can change it

Customer emails are part of the premium plan. Only admins can change the switches. Everyone else sees the page read-only.

## Team notifications are separate

Emails to your own team are a different thing. Each member manages those on their personal notifications page. They come in these groups: Billing, Usage, Integrations, Security, System, Team, Getting started and Product news.
