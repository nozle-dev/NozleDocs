---
title: Customer emails
description: Choose which emails Nozle sends to your customers, and where the buttons in them lead.
---

Customer emails are the emails Nozle sends to your customers, in your brand, on your behalf. Open **Settings** and choose **Customer emails**.

Each [billing entity](/guides/settings/billing-entities) has its own switches. Emails go out from Nozle's no-reply address, and replies go to the billing email of the entity. If the entity has no billing email, Nozle sends nothing, whatever is switched on. Every email is off until you turn it on. **Turn on recommended** switches on the ones marked Recommended.

## The emails

| Group | Email |
| --- | --- |
| Billing documents | Invoice finalized, Credit note issued, Payment receipt |
| Payments | Payment failed, Payment needs confirmation |
| Usage and credits | Credits used up, Usage alert, Usage threshold reached |
| Subscription | Trial ending, Renewal reminder, Price change scheduled, Subscription started, Subscription ended |

Payment receipt needs the issue receipts add-on. Without it the switch stays off. **Send test** sends a sample of an email to you.

## Customer billing page

**Customer billing page** is the address the buttons in your emails open. Enter a full address that starts with `https://`. Emails marked **Billing page** have no button until you set it, except Credits used up, which falls back to the hosted portal. Payment buttons open the payment provider's page first.

## Who can change it

Customer emails are part of the premium plan. Only admins can change the switches. Everyone else sees the page read-only.

## Team notifications are separate

Emails to your own team are a different thing. Each member manages those on their personal notifications page. They come in these groups: Billing, Usage, Integrations, Security, System, Team, Getting started and Product news.
