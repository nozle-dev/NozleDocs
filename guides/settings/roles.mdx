---
title: Roles and capabilities
description: The built-in roles that decide what each member can see and change.
---

A role is a set of capabilities. You give each member one or more roles, and they get the union of what those roles allow. See [Team access](/guides/settings/team-access).

## Built-in roles

Nozle ships three roles:

| Role | Code | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | `admin` | Administrator with all capabilities. |
| Finance | `finance` | Manages financial data. |
| Manager | `manager` | The predefined manager role. |

These roles are fixed. You cannot edit or delete them, and you cannot create your own roles.

## The roles page

Open **Settings** and choose **Roles**. For each role the page shows:

- its code,
- how many capabilities it grants,
- how many members hold it.

Viewing the page needs the `settings:read` capability. Roles are assigned to people on the [Members page](/guides/settings/team-access).
