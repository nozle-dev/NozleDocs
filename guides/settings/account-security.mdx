title: Account security

These settings are personal. They apply to your own sign-in, not to the workspace.

## Profile

Your email is used to sign in and to attribute every change you make.

## Password

Nozle changes passwords through a verified email link, never in the browser. Choose **Send reset link** and follow the link in the email. Your current password keeps working until you use the link. Resetting your password signs your other browsers out.

## Active sessions

Every browser and device with a valid session is listed.

- **Revoke** ends one session. Revoking the one you are using signs you out.
- **Revoke every other session** signs out every device except this one. It cannot be undone.

A session ends when its token expires, when you revoke it, or when you reset your password.

## Sign-in history

The history lists your recent sign-ins and marks the unsuccessful ones. If several failed attempts happened just before your last successful sign-in, the page warns you. Reset your password if that was not you.

If your role cannot view the security log, the history is hidden. The security log is available in every workspace. Admins can see everyone's sign-ins under [Audit logs](/guides/settings/audit-logs).

## Sign out

Signing out ends this session and returns you to the sign-in screen. Every other open tab signs out too. Your data and workspace access stay as they were.
