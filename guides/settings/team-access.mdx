title: Team access
description: Invite people to your workspace, give them roles, and remove them.
---

Open **Settings** and choose **Members** to see who has access to your workspace, and who has been invited.

## Invite someone

1. Choose **Invite member**.
2. Enter their work email.
3. Pick at least one [role](/guides/settings/roles). An invitation without a role is refused.

Inviting needs settings access. A member gets the union of the capabilities of all their roles. Choosing **Admin** selects only Admin, because it already grants every capability.

## Invitations

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Sent and not yet accepted. |
| Accepted | The person joined. |
| Expired | Seven days passed since it was last sent. |
| Revoked | You cancelled it. |
| Delivery failed | The email provider reported a bounce or a rejected address. |

- **Expiry.** An invitation expires seven days after it was last sent.
- **Resend.** Sends the same invitation again and refreshes the expiry. It does not create a second invitation. After a send you wait about a minute before resending. Accepted and revoked invitations cannot be resent.
- **Revoke.** The emailed link stops working. You can invite the address again at any time.

## Change roles

Changing a member's roles needs workspace admin access. Every member needs at least one role.

## Remove a member

Removing a member ends their access at once. Their history and audit records stay.

A few guards protect you from locking yourself out:

- The workspace owner always keeps administrator access and cannot be removed. Transfer ownership first.
- You cannot remove the last administrator. Promote someone else first.
- You cannot remove your own access or your own administrator access. Ask another administrator.
