---
title: Billing entities
description: Set up the legal entities that issue your invoices.
---

A billing entity is a legal entity that issues invoices. It decides what a customer document says: who issued it, how it is numbered, and when it is due. It does not decide which data an API key can reach. That is an [environment](/guides/settings/environments).

Open **Settings** and choose **Billing entities**. You can also manage them with the API: [list](/api-reference/billing-entities/list-all-billing-entities), [create](/api-reference/billing-entities/create-a-billing-entity), [get](/api-reference/billing-entities/retrieve-a-billing-entity) and [update](/api-reference/billing-entities/update-a-billing-entity).

## Fields

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Name, code, billing email, logo (PNG or JPEG, up to 800 KB) |
| Legal | Legal name, legal number, tax identification number |
| Address | Address lines, city, state or region, postcode, country as a 2-letter code |
| Currency and time | Currency, timezone |
| Documents | Document language, document number prefix (up to 10 characters), numbering scheme, invoice footer |
| Payment | Net payment term (days), invoice grace period (days) |
| Issuing | Invoice issuing date anchor (Current period end or Next period start), issuing date adjustment (Align with finalization date or Keep anchor) |
| Switches | Finalize zero-amount invoices, EU tax management, electronic invoicing (shown for countries that require it) |
| Also | Customer emails, taxes, invoice custom sections |

The numbering scheme is **Per customer** or **Per billing entity**. Customer email switches live on [Customer emails](/guides/settings/customer-emails). To pick custom sections, save the entity first, then reopen it.

## The default entity

Your workspace has a default billing entity. Edits to it apply from the next document it issues. Documents already issued do not change.

## More than one entity

Extra billing entities depend on your plan and organization. You also need billing entity creation permission. If more are not available, the page says so and the **New billing entity** button is disabled.
