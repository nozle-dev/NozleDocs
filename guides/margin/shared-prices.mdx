---
title: Shared prices
description: A price list of provider models that Nozle keeps for every workspace, and how to adopt a price
---

Every LLM model needs a price per token bucket before Nozle can compute your cost. Instead of typing the same provider prices in every workspace, Nozle keeps a **shared price list** of provider models. You read it, you can adopt a price into your own catalog with one click, and your own rates always win.

## What is on the list

Each entry is one revision of one provider model and has:

- the provider, the model and the other names the provider reports for it (aliases such as dated snapshots)
- a price per token for each bucket: `input`, `cached_input`, `cache_write`, `cache_write_1h`, `output`, `reasoning`, `audio_input`, `audio_output`, `image_input`, `image_output`, `batch_input`, `batch_output` (the same buckets as [token buckets](/guides/ai/token-billing#token-buckets))
- the currency, and the dates the price is in force
- the **source page** and the **day Nozle read the price there**

The list holds only prices that Nozle read on the provider's own pricing page. A model or price that could not be verified is not listed. A change is a new revision; the one before it is closed and stays readable. Nozle staff maintain the list in the admin console. Your workspace cannot change it.

Open **Shared prices** in the dashboard, or query `nozleSharedProviderPrices` (GraphQL, `cost_intelligence:view`). Prices are shown per one million tokens in the dashboard and per token in the API.

## How a price is chosen

For one model at one moment, Nozle uses this order, every time:

1. **Your own catalog.** The catalog version of your workspace in force at that moment whose model or alias is the model.
2. **The shared list.** The shared revision chosen the same way.
3. **Per bucket.** Your rate wins for every bucket your catalog has. The shared price only fills a bucket your catalog has no rate for, and only in the same currency. A shared price that differs from yours is shown as "yours differs" and is never applied.

Within one list, an exact model name beats an alias, then the later start date wins, then the higher version number.

<Note>
The shared list never changes a rate you already have. It also does not price events by itself: a shared price starts to count only after you adopt it and a Cost Sheet component uses it.
</Note>

You can read the resolved price of a model, bucket by bucket with where each rate comes from (`organization` or `shared`), with `nozleResolvedProviderPrice(provider, model)`.

## Adopt a shared price

When a model is in the [Uncosted models](/guides/ai/token-billing#uncosted-models) queue and the shared list has a price for it, the row shows **Adopt shared price**. One click:

1. copies the shared price into your own catalog (`nozleAdoptSharedProviderPrice`), with a link back to the revision it came from, and
2. opens the new Cost Sheet form with the provider, the model and the rates already filled in, so you choose the feature and publish.

Adoption is refused when your workspace already has a catalog for the model (`already_priced_by_organization`), so an existing rate is never replaced. After you publish, use [Reprocess](/guides/margin/reprocess) to price the events that were already recorded.

Your copy is yours: later changes to the shared list do not change it. To follow a new shared price, create a new catalog version.
