---
title: Shared prices
description: The provider price list Nozle keeps for every workspace, so LLM calls are costed even for models you have not priced yourself
---

Your margin shows the cost of LLM calls from the first call, even for a model you never priced. Nozle keeps a **shared price list** of provider models, and a model without a price of your own is costed from it automatically. Your own prices always win. There is nothing to turn on and no adopt step (Core 11.43.0, Engine 2.58.0).

## What is on the list

Each entry is one revision of one provider model and has:

- the provider, the model and the other names the provider reports for it (aliases such as dated snapshots)
- a price per token for each bucket: `input`, `cached_input`, `cache_write`, `cache_write_1h`, `output`, `reasoning`, `audio_input`, `audio_output`, `image_input`, `image_output`, `batch_input`, `batch_output` (the same buckets as [token buckets](/guides/ai/token-billing#token-buckets))
- the currency, and the dates the price is in force
- the **source page** and the **day Nozle read the price there**

Prices come from two places:

- **Provider pricing pages.** Nozle staff read them and maintain these revisions in the admin console.
- **models.dev.** Three times a day, at 03:30, 11:30 and 19:30 UTC, Nozle imports the models of every provider [models.dev](https://models.dev) lists (Core 11.52.0; before, only OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini). Each provider is imported under its models.dev provider id, for example `groq`, `openrouter`, `togetherai`, `amazon-bedrock`, `azure` or `google-vertex`; Google AI Studio is `gemini`. A model that several providers list is stored once per provider, each at that provider's price. A model is imported only when every price models.dev lists maps to a token bucket and its only output is text. Models with prompt-size tiers, image or audio output, or no price are left out. models.dev has no batch or 1 hour cache write rates, so imported revisions have no `batch_input`, `batch_output` or `cache_write_1h`. Each imported revision cites models.dev as its source.

Your workspace cannot change the list.

Open **Settings > Shared prices** in the dashboard. The list is paged: choose a provider and type the start of a model name to find a price. Through GraphQL (`cost_intelligence:view`), `nozleSharedProviderPrices` takes `page` and `limit`, filters by an exact `provider` and by `modelPrefix` (the start of the model name, ignoring case), and answers `collection` and `metadata` like other lists. `nozleSharedProviderPriceProviders` lists the provider names. Before Core 11.52.0, `nozleSharedProviderPrices` answered the whole list at once. Prices are shown per one million tokens in the dashboard and per token in the API.

## Which provider prices a call

A call is priced at the vendor that bills it, named by the API host it went to (Core 11.52.0, Engine 2.60.0).

Usage capture reports the API format as `provider` (`openai`, `anthropic` or `gemini`) and the host the request went to as `provider_host`. A call to Groq, Together, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, Fireworks or Azure made with the OpenAI client reports `openai`, and a Bedrock or Vertex call made with the Anthropic client reports `anthropic`. Nozle reads the host to find the real vendor:

1. The host is lowercased and its port dropped.
2. An exact entry for the host wins. Otherwise the longest suffix entry that matches wins, when its prefix matches too: `bedrock-runtime.us-east-1.amazonaws.com` is `amazon-bedrock`, `*.openai.azure.com` is `azure` and `*-aiplatform.googleapis.com` is `google-vertex`.
3. With no entry, the reported `provider` stays.

Nozle keeps the host list. It is filled from the base URL of every models.dev provider whose host no other provider uses, plus a list Nozle maintains for the main vendors.

The vendor found this way chooses the shared price, decides whether a [provider is required](#when-the-call-names-no-provider), and names the provider of an [uncosted model](/guides/ai/token-billing#uncosted-models) row, so the dashboard shows the real vendor. A call without `provider_host` keeps its reported provider. Uncosted rows recorded before Engine 2.60.0 keep the reported provider, for example `openai` for a Groq call.

<Note>
Your own prices and Cost Sheet conditions still match the `provider` the call reported, not the vendor from the host. To price a Groq call made with the OpenAI client yourself, the condition's `provider` is `openai` and its `model` is the Groq model name.
</Note>

## When the call names no provider

A call that reports no `provider` (an SDK event without it, for example) is matched against the model in every provider's list.

- When every provider that lists the model, by name or alias at the call's time, has the same prices, that price applies.
- When their prices differ, there is no shared price: `gpt-4o` is listed by `openai` and by other providers that resell it at another price. The call is uncosted with the reason `provider_required` (the `failure_code` of the Cost or Feature Event, and the `last_failure_code` of the uncosted model row). The dashboard shows it as **Provider needed**. Adopting the shared price of such a row is refused with `model: ["provider_required"]`.

To fix it, send `provider` (and `provider_host`) with the event, or [add your own price](#add-your-own-price) for the model. Then [reprocess](/guides/margin/reprocess): reprocessing an uncosted model also re-rates its `provider_required` events.

Your own prices are matched the same way: a call without a provider matches your own price of the model under any provider (Core 11.52.0).

## When a price changes

A price change never rewrites history.

- A change is a new revision from the day it was read. The revision before it is closed and stays readable, so past events keep the price they were costed at.
- A price starts on the day Nozle read it. Events before that day are not repriced from it.
- When models.dev changes the price of a model it imported, the importer publishes a new revision. A model or alias with a revision from Nozle staff or from Nozle's starter list is never changed by the import.

## How a price is chosen

For one model at one moment, Nozle uses this order, every time:

1. **Your own catalog.** The catalog version of your workspace in force at that moment whose model or alias is the model.
2. **The shared list.** The shared revision chosen the same way.
3. **Per bucket.** Your rate wins for every bucket your catalog has. The shared price only fills a bucket your catalog has no rate for, and only in the same currency. A shared price that differs from yours is shown as "yours differs" and is never applied.

Within one list, an exact model name beats an alias, then the later start date wins, then the higher version number.

<Note>
The shared list never overrides a rate you have. It rates an event only when none of your cost rules matches it. See [shared price fallback](#shared-price-fallback).
</Note>

You can read the resolved price of a model, bucket by bucket with where each rate comes from (`organization` or `shared`), with `nozleResolvedProviderPrice(provider, model)`. Pass `providerHost` to resolve the vendor from the host as a call would.

## Shared price fallback

When an LLM event has no matching cost rule of yours, Nozle rates it from the shared price list, per token bucket, instead of leaving it uncosted:

- The cost line carries `price_source: "shared"` (`"org"` for your own rules). It shows in the [cost report](/api/cost-intelligence-report#price-source) and in margin.
- Your own rates are never overridden. An event that one of your rules matches is rated by your rule.
- The shared price must be in force when the event happened. An event dated before the price's `effective_from` gets no shared price and stays uncosted.
- If the shared price lacks a price for one of the buckets the event used, the whole event stays uncosted. It is never costed in part.
- In the [Uncosted models](/guides/ai/token-billing#uncosted-models) list, `shared_price_available` is true on a row when the list prices that model now.

The fallback does not copy anything into your catalog.

## Add your own price

Your own price always wins over the shared list. Add one when a model is missing from the list, when a call names no provider and the shared prices of its model differ, or when you pay a negotiated rate.

- **A Cost Sheet.** Create a cost model with the provider, the model and a rate per token bucket. See [cost models](/guides/margin/cost-models) and [set up AI token billing](/guides/ai/token-billing#2-record-the-cost-of-tokens-and-see-margin). The conditions match the `provider` the call reports.
- **From the shared list.** In the Uncosted queue, adopt the shared price, below, and publish it as your own.

Then [reprocess](/guides/margin/reprocess) the events that were recorded before the price existed.

## Adopt a shared price

Adopting is optional. Do it when you want to keep a price as your own, so later changes to the shared list do not change it.

When a model is in the [Uncosted models](/guides/ai/token-billing#uncosted-models) queue and the shared list has a price for it, the row shows **Adopt shared price**. One click:

1. copies the shared price into your own catalog (`nozleAdoptSharedProviderPrice`), with a link back to the revision it came from, and
2. opens the new Cost Sheet form with the provider, the model and the rates already filled in, so you choose the feature and publish.

Adoption is refused when your workspace already has a catalog for the model (`already_priced_by_organization`), so an existing rate is never replaced. After you publish, use [Reprocess](/guides/margin/reprocess) to price the events that were already recorded.

## Refuse calls to unpriced models

Set `uncosted_model_action` to `deny` (GraphQL `billingConfiguration.uncostedModelAction`) to stop calls to a model that nothing prices. A model is priced when you have a cost rule for it or the shared list has a price in force at that moment.

| Action | Model with no cost rule and no shared price |
|---|---|
| `warn` (default) | The call runs, its cost counts as zero, you are notified. |
| `ignore` | The call runs, no notification. |
| `deny` | The call is refused before it runs. |

- **`GET /can`.** Send the optional `model` and `provider` query parameters. A refused call answers `allowed: false`, `reason: "uncosted_model_denied"`, with `model` and `message`. Without `model` nothing is checked. See [Check an entitlement](/api/check-entitlement).
- **LLM proxy.** The model is read from the request body. A refused call answers `403` with code `uncosted_model_denied` and the provider is not called. See [LLM proxy errors](/api/llm-proxy#errors).
- **When the price cannot be looked up.** `/can` follows the `on_unavailable` of the margin policy that applies (the last one known for the call), and allows the call when none is known. The proxy allows the call and marks the response `X-Nozle-Budget-Unverified: true`. See [when the policy cannot be read](/guides/margin/alerts#when-the-policy-cannot-be-read).

Because a shared price counts as a price, a model on the shared list is not refused, and its events are rated from the list.
