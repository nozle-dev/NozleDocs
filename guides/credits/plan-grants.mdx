---
title: Included Plan Credits
description: Configure recurring customer grants and lifecycle behavior
---

Plan credit grant rules attach an amount and cadence to one Credit System. Cadence can be monthly, yearly, multi-period, or lifetime and is independent from invoice frequency.

For each entitlement period, Nozle creates a distinct balance source with a deterministic reference. It never resets one shared mutable balance row.

## Grant readiness

Initial grants are asynchronous. The hosted scheduler checks for grants once per minute; an active subscription can temporarily have no spendable credit balance. Poll the expected Credit System balance with bounded backoff before allowing credit-backed work. Subscription activation alone is not confirmation that its credits are available.

## Lifecycle policy

- **Activation:** grant the configured target.
- **Immediate upgrade:** grant `max(target - cumulative amount already granted for the same period and subscription lineage, 0)` per Credit System.
- **Downgrade:** do not claw back; the lower target applies next period.
- **Cycle-end transition:** create no immediate difference grant.
- **Cancellation:** subscription sources expire at their own period boundary; lifetime sources retain their configured lifetime behavior.
- **Cadence change:** a plan change that grants a Credit System on another cadence, for example monthly to yearly inside a plan family, grants the new rule's full amount once, starting at the new subscription's billing anchor. Credits of the old cadence are removed only by the plan change's own revocations. A Credit System whose cadence does not change follows the upgrade rule above.
- **Cancel and subscribe again:** the cancelled subscription's plan credits stay spendable until they expire. A new subscription for the same customer (or Entity) grants only the part of its period that those unexpired credits do not cover, prorated by time. For example, resubscribing to a 100-credit monthly plan in the same calendar month leaves 100 credits for that month, not 200. Credits already spent on the cancelled subscription do not increase the new grant.
- **Lifetime credits:** granted once per customer (or Entity) and Credit System. Cancelling and subscribing again does not grant them again.

Plan changes inside one subscription, resubscribing after the cancelled credits expired, and other subscriptions that are still active (add-ons, Entity subscriptions) are not reduced by the resubscribe rule.

Each Credit System is granted on its own: a grant that fails for one Credit System does not hold back the grants of the others on the same subscription.

Paid top-ups remain separate sources and are not reduced by plan changes.
