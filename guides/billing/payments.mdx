---
title: Payments
description: Payment processing, retries, and dunning
---

Nozle collects payments through the provider connected to your organization: Stripe or Razorpay. Payments are initiated during checkout and processed automatically for recurring subscriptions.

**Payment flow:**

1. Customer pays in the provider's UI: the Stripe-hosted Checkout page, or the Razorpay checkout modal or payment link
2. The provider processes the payment
3. The provider sends a signed webhook to Nozle. Stripe sends `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed` or `payment_intent.canceled`. Razorpay sends `payment.authorized`, `payment.captured`, `payment.failed`, `order.paid` or `payment_link.paid`. For a Razorpay order, your backend also calls verify with the handler's signature
4. Nozle updates invoice payment status
5. If the invoice belongs to a payment-gated plan checkout, Nozle activates or cancels the incomplete subscription

See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout) for the webhook setup and the Stripe and Razorpay flows, and the [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) and [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay) integration guides to connect a provider.

**Payment methods:**

The PaymentMethodDisplay component shows the customer's saved card:

```tsx
import { PaymentMethodDisplay } from '@nozle-js/react'

<PaymentMethodDisplay
  paymentMethod={{ last4: '4242', brand: 'visa', expMonth: 12, expYear: 2028 }}
  onUpdatePaymentMethod={() => router.push('/update-card')}
/>
```

**Receipt link:**

For a Stripe payment, the GraphQL `Payment.receiptUrl` field returns Stripe's receipt page for the payment. Nozle fetches the link from Stripe when you ask for it and never stores it, so it is not available later from Nozle's own records.

**Failed payments and retries:**

When a payment fails, Nozle records the failed payment status. Dunning and retries can be configured for recurring invoices:

- Automatic dunning: configurable retry schedule. It is part of the Growth, Scale and Enterprise [Nozle plans](/guides/getting-started/nozle-plans#premium-features). The hourly run creates a payment request for each overdue invoice of a customer with a campaign, on the campaign's schedule: it retries the payment with the customer's payment provider, or emails the customer the payment link when there is none. A customer or billing entity can use only a dunning campaign of your own organization. For a customer who pays through Razorpay the payment link goes out by email, so the billing entity needs an email: without one, the payment request is refused with `billing_entity_email_missing` instead of being created for nobody.
- Dunning policy (_Unreleased_): a campaign with an end action follows a timeline over the plan's grace days instead of the attempt cadence, and blocks the customer's usage when the grace ends. See [Dunning policy](/guides/billing/dunning).
- Manual dunning: manually trigger retry
- Amounts below the payment provider's minimum charge (_Unreleased_) are not charged or dunned: they are carried to the customer's next invoice. See [Amounts below the provider minimum](/guides/billing/invoices#amounts-below-the-provider-minimum).
- Pay now and pay links are refused with `payment_already_processing` while an automatic debit of the invoice is waiting for the bank. See [Pay links](/guides/billing/checkout#pay-links).

**Product credit top-ups:**

Configure fixed top-up packages for a Credit System. Checkout creation produces an invoice/payment flow; credits are granted once the top-up invoice is fully paid, whether by the provider, a manual payment for the full amount, a payment request or marking it paid:

Expose top-up packages through your authenticated backend, start checkout from your billing UI, and refresh the balance only after the invoice is paid. Product credits are separate from invoice wallet credits. See [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups).

For related configuration, see [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout), [Subscriptions](/guides/billing/subscriptions), and [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups).
