Nozle collects payments through the provider connected to your organization: Stripe or Razorpay. Payments are initiated during checkout and processed automatically for recurring subscriptions.

**Payment flow:**

1. Customer pays in the provider's UI: the Stripe-hosted Checkout page, or the Razorpay checkout modal or payment link
2. The provider processes the payment
3. The provider sends a signed webhook to Nozle. Stripe sends `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed` or `payment_intent.canceled`. Razorpay sends `payment.authorized`, `payment.captured`, `payment.failed`, `order.paid` or `payment_link.paid`. For a Razorpay order, your backend also calls verify with the handler's signature
4. Nozle updates invoice payment status
5. If the invoice belongs to a payment-gated plan checkout, Nozle activates or cancels the incomplete subscription

See [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout) for the webhook setup and the Stripe and Razorpay flows, and the [Stripe](/integrations/stripe) and [Razorpay](/integrations/razorpay) integration guides to connect a provider.

**Payment methods:**

The PaymentMethodDisplay component shows the customer's saved card:

```tsx
import { PaymentMethodDisplay } from '@nozle-js/react'

<PaymentMethodDisplay
  paymentMethod={{ last4: '4242', brand: 'visa', expMonth: 12, expYear: 2028 }}
  onUpdatePaymentMethod={() => router.push('/update-card')}
/>
```

**Receipt link:**

For a Stripe payment, the GraphQL `Payment.receiptUrl` field returns Stripe's receipt page for the payment. Nozle fetches the link from Stripe when you ask for it and never stores it, so it is not available later from Nozle's own records.

**Failed payments and retries:**

When a payment fails, Nozle records the failed payment status. Dunning and retries can be configured for recurring invoices:

- Automatic dunning: configurable retry schedule. It works on every [Nozle plan](/guides/getting-started/nozle-plans#premium-features), and a new workspace starts with a [default dunning policy](/guides/billing/dunning#the-default-policy). The hourly run creates a payment request for each overdue invoice of a customer with a campaign, on the campaign's schedule: it retries the payment with the customer's payment provider, or emails the customer the payment link when there is none. A customer or billing entity can use only a dunning campaign of your own organization. For a customer who pays through Razorpay the payment link goes out by email, so the billing entity needs an email: without one, the payment request is refused with `billing_entity_email_missing` instead of being created for nobody.
- Dunning policy: a campaign with an end action follows a timeline over the plan's grace days instead of the attempt cadence, and blocks the customer's usage when the grace ends. See [Dunning policy](/guides/billing/dunning).
- Manual dunning: manually trigger retry
- Amounts below the payment provider's minimum charge are not charged or dunned: they are carried to the customer's next invoice. See [Amounts below the provider minimum](/guides/billing/invoices#amounts-below-the-provider-minimum).
- Pay now and pay links are refused with `payment_already_processing` while an automatic debit of the invoice is waiting for the bank. See [Pay links](/guides/billing/checkout#pay-links).

**Product credit top-ups:**

Configure fixed top-up packages for a Credit System. Checkout creation produces an invoice/payment flow; credits are granted once the top-up invoice is fully paid, whether by the provider, a manual payment for the full amount, a payment request or marking it paid:

Expose top-up packages through your authenticated backend, start checkout from your billing UI, and refresh the balance only after the invoice is paid. Product credits are separate from invoice wallet credits. See [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups).

For related configuration, see [Checkout](/guides/billing/checkout), [Subscriptions](/guides/billing/subscriptions), and [Paid Credit Top-ups](/guides/credits/top-ups).

## Payment receipts

_Core 11.44.0._ Every Nozle plan issues a payment receipt for each successful payment, so your customers get proof of payment without any setup. Before, receipts needed the Scale plan.

- The receipt email (`payment_receipt.created`) is on by default for a billing entity created from Core 11.44.0. Billing entities created before keep the setting they had.
- Switch it off, or on, in **Settings > Customer emails**. See [Customer emails](/guides/settings/customer-emails).
- The `payment_receipt.created` and `payment_receipt.generated` webhooks are sent as before. See [Webhook events](/api/webhook-events).
- A sandbox creates receipts but never emails them.

## Refunds

You refund a paid invoice with a credit note, in the dashboard or the API. The refund goes back to the original payment method, or you record money you already paid back yourself (see [Refunded outside Nozle](#refunded-outside-nozle)). When nothing can be refunded, the dashboard's credit note form says why (dashboard 2.21.0). It shows one of these reasons.

- The invoice was below the payment provider's minimum charge and was [carried forward](/guides/billing/invoices#amounts-below-the-provider-minimum) to the next invoice. Nothing was charged.
- The invoice has not been paid.
- Everything that can go back to the original payment method was refunded already. This includes an invoice whose payment was [lost in a dispute](/guides/billing/dunning#lost-disputes), because the bank already took that money back.
- No refundable payment is recorded yet. If the invoice was just paid, choose **Refresh** in a moment.

In the first three cases the form suggests crediting the amount to the customer's balance instead. The form also shows the most that can be refunded, and flags a refund above it.

### Where an online refund goes

_Core 11.46.0._ The online part of a refund (`refund_amount_cents` minus `out_of_band_refund_amount_cents`) goes to the invoice's succeeded payments through Stripe, GoCardless, Adyen or Razorpay, oldest first. Duplicate payments are skipped. Each payment takes at most what it still holds, so a refund larger than one payment is split into one provider refund per payment. When the invoice was paid through a payment request and has no payment of its own, the request's payments are used.

- The refund is `succeeded` once every part succeeded, and `failed` once one part failed.
- If the payments cannot take the online part, the credit note is refused and nothing is sent. The error code, `manual_refund_required`, `provider_refund_unsupported` or `insufficient_refundable_amount`, says why. See [Refund errors](/api/errors#refund-errors).
- A credit note Nozle creates itself, when a subscription is terminated, is not refused. If no payment can take its refund, its `refund_status` is `failed` at once, nothing is sent and the `credit_note.refund_failure` webhook names the error code.

Before Core 11.46.0 a refund went to the invoice's newest payment of any kind. A newer manual or failed payment left the refund pending, and a refund larger than the newest payment was rejected by the provider.

### When a refund fails

_Core 11.46.0._ A refund that fails at the payment provider gives the amount back to the invoice, so you can refund it again, for example out of band. The invoice's `refundable_amount_cents` and the amounts left to credit no longer count it. The parts that did not fail and the out-of-band part still count.

- You get the `credit_note.refund_failure` webhook. See [Webhook events](/api/webhook-events#credit_noterefund_failure).
- A refund the provider rejects at once, for example an amount above what the Stripe payment still holds, takes nothing from the invoice either.
- The dashboard shows the credit note as **Refund failed**, with a note that the amount can be refunded again (dashboard 2.22.0).

### Refunded outside Nozle

_Core 11.46.0, dashboard 2.22.0._ Use this when you paid the customer back yourself, for example by bank transfer or in cash. Nozle records the amount as refunded and never sends it to a payment provider. It is the way to refund an invoice that:

- was paid with a manual payment, or was only marked paid. An invoice marked paid with no payment recorded can be refunded this way up to its total. Before, it had nothing to refund.
- was paid through Cashfree, Flutterwave or Moneyhash, which Nozle cannot send refunds to.

On a credit note, three fields record it:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `out_of_band_refund_amount_cents` | The part of `refund_amount_cents` you paid back yourself. 0 by default, and at most `refund_amount_cents`. |
| `out_of_band_refund_reference` | Your own reference, for example a bank transfer ID. Optional, up to 255 characters. |
| `out_of_band_refunded_at` | When you paid it back. The time the credit note is created when you send none. |

A credit note refunded only out of band has `refund_status` `succeeded` at once. The fields are on the credit note in the API and in the credit note webhooks.

In the dashboard, the credit note form has **Refunded outside Nozle** next to **Refund to the original payment method**, with an optional reference and the day of the refund (today by default, never a future day). When the invoice has no succeeded payment through Stripe, GoCardless, Adyen or Razorpay, the form hides the original payment method field and says why.

To void a paid invoice with a refund, send `generate_credit_note: true`, `refund_amount` and `out_of_band_refund_amount` to `POST /invoices/{id}/void`. It is the out-of-band part of the refund on the credit note the void creates. A manually paid invoice is voided with a refund this way.

An invoice [carried forward](/guides/billing/invoices#amounts-below-the-provider-minimum) because it was below the provider's minimum charge has nothing to refund, online or out of band.
