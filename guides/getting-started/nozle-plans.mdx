Your workspace is on one of Nozle's plans. Prices match [nozle.dev/pricing](https://nozle.dev/pricing). See and change your plan in the dashboard under **Settings > Billing settings > Plan**.

These plans and limits apply only to your workspace. They never limit or bill your own customers: you set their plans, limits and alerts yourself.

## Events over the included amount

Nozle counts the events your workspace processes in each billing period. The count is reported to your plan every hour.

- Events over the included amount are never blocked. They are billed as overage at $0.04 per 1,000 events at the end of the month.
- Overage is billed in blocks of 1,000 events. A started block counts as a full block.
- Example: 60,000 events on Starter are 10,000 over the included 50,000, so 10 blocks: $0.40.
- After a plan change in the middle of a month, every event is billed once: events before the change on the old plan's final invoice, events after it on the new plan.
- Your Nozle invoice stays a draft for one day after the period ends. Events that reach Nozle late but belong to the period are billed on that invoice while it is a draft, and on your next invoice after that. For the same reason, the Growth and Scale fee of a renewal is collected one day later.

## Revenue share

Growth and Scale charge a share of the revenue you collect through Nozle in the billing period: 0.6% on Growth, 0.5% on Scale. Starter has no revenue share.

- Collected revenue is your paid invoices, excluding tax, minus refunds. An invoice counts through its successful payments, or once as marked paid when it has no successful payment, never both.
- Each payment or refund is converted to USD at the rate of its date: the official peg for AED, SAR, QAR, BHD, OMR and JOD, otherwise the ECB reference rate. Currencies the ECB does not publish use a second daily public source. A payment in a currency with no rate yet waits until a rate exists; a rate is never guessed.
- Example: $10,000 collected on Growth adds $60.00.

## MRR limit and automatic upgrade

Each plan tracks your MRR against its limit: $5K on Starter, $100K on Growth, $1M on Scale. Tracked MRR is the MRR of your active subscriptions in every currency, converted to USD at the latest ECB rates. It is used only for the limit; the revenue share is charged on collected revenue.

Nozle checks the limit once a day. When your tracked MRR passes it, your workspace is upgraded to the next plan automatically:

- Starter goes to Growth, and Growth goes to Scale. This is the same plan change as the upgrade under **Settings > Billing settings > Plan**: it applies now, prorated, and unused prepaid time is credited. An upgrade from Starter bills any unpaid overage and the prorated new fee on one invoice.
- With a saved payment method, the change is charged and applied at once. Without one, the upgrade waits until its checkout is paid.
- The premium features of the new plan switch on with it.
- There is no automatic downgrade.
- Scale over $1M MRR stays on Scale. [Contact sales](mailto:sales@nozle.dev) for Enterprise.

Emails when you reach 50%, 80%, 90% and 100% of your included events or MRR limit are coming later.

## Usage on the Plan page

**Settings > Billing settings > Plan** shows your current plan and two usage bars:

- **Events used this period**: events used against the events included in your plan.
- **MRR tracked**: tracked MRR in USD against your plan's MRR limit.

At 80% of your included events, a banner shows the percentage used. On Starter and Growth, a note says that passing the MRR limit upgrades the workspace automatically. A bar reads **Not reported** when Nozle has no number for it yet; it never shows 0% for a missing number. After a plan change in the middle of a period, the events bar keeps counting from the start of the period.

### Plan status fields

The dashboard reads the bars from your workspace's plan status (GraphQL `nozlePlanStatus`, from Nozle's billing status of your workspace). Besides the plan, it carries:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `events_used` | Events reported in the current billing period, across a plan change in that period. |
| `events_included` | Events included in your plan. |
| `events_used_percent` | `events_used` / `events_included`, one decimal. |
| `mrr_tracked_usd_cents` | Tracked MRR of every currency, in USD cents at the latest ECB rates. |
| `mrr_limit_usd_cents` | Your plan's MRR limit, in USD cents. |
| `mrr_used_percent` | `mrr_tracked_usd_cents` / `mrr_limit_usd_cents`, one decimal. |
| `plan_limits` | `{events, mrr_cents}`: the plan's included events and MRR limit in USD cents. |

Limits and percentages are `null` when the plan has none, and the MRR values are `null` while a currency has no rate. These fields describe your workspace only. The [billing status](/api/billing-status) of your own customers never includes them.
